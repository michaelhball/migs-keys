# Sound sources

Acoustic piano samples: Salamander Grand Piano, recorded by Alexander Holm,
Yamaha C5. Distributed under Creative Commons Attribution 3.0:
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/

Source of the MP3 subset: https://github.com/Tonejs/audio/tree/master/salamander
The upstream README is retained in public/audio/piano/README.txt. The MP3
files are unchanged. The player transposes nearby recorded pitches and applies
its own note-release envelope. It uses one velocity layer, not the complete
Salamander library.

Electric piano, organ and synth pad are synthesized locally by this app.
