### Free intro transcription

[Open the one-page intro score (PDF)](https://killersolos.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2010/03/outkast_roses_piano_solo.pdf)

This is a transcription of the ten-bar piano introduction, rather than a chord sheet for the rest of the song. The author includes an audio comparison and notes that the transcription may contain inaccuracies. It still needs a note-by-note check before use in our player.

[Transcriber's page and audio comparison](https://killersolos.wordpress.com/2010/03/29/outkast-roses-piano-solo-with-sheet-music/)

### Video lesson

[Watch HDpiano's free first intro lesson](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=za6SN-5TNVU)

The [HDpiano lesson page](https://hdpiano.com/lesson/roses-by-outkast) separates the intro into two parts and lists the song in E minor at 109 BPM. The first lesson is public; the remaining lessons require a subscription.

### Published score

[Musicnotes: Roses, piano/vocal/guitar edition](https://www.musicnotes.com/sheetmusic/outkast/roses/MN0095908)

This is a paid, ten-page edition of the whole song, in E minor, with a metronome marking of quarter note = 108. Preview it before deciding whether to buy it.
