# Source and arrangement decisions

Source: the learner-supplied copy of [Pianote's piano tutorial](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=amRtNe2uVwE). The source video and extracted frames remain outside Git. The repository contains the transcription/build code and generated practice assets, as requested.

Review used sparse score frames, not frame-by-frame video processing. Useful teaching frames: 00:30 (intro bars 1–4), 05:30 (5–8), 13:30 (41–44), 14:30 (45–48), 15:30 (53–56). The final playthrough was checked at approximately eight-second intervals, with additional section checks: 16:30 (1–4), 16:38 (5–8), 16:46 (9–12), 16:54 (13–16), 17:00 (17–20), 17:10 (21–24), 17:18 (25–28), 17:30 (29–32), 17:34 (33–36), 17:42 (37–40), 18:00 (45–48), 18:06 (49–52), 18:14 (53–56), 18:22 (57–60), 18:30 (61–64), 18:38 (65–68), 18:46 (69–72), 18:54 (73–76), 19:00 (77–80), 19:10 (81–84), 19:18 (85–88), 19:30 (89–92), 19:34 (93–96), 19:42 (97–100), 19:50 (101–104), 20:00 (105–108), 20:06 (109–112), 20:14 (113–116).

This is a **practice arrangement of the piano accompaniment**, following that 116-bar form. It retains the E-major riff, walking bass, syncopated chord section, transitions and chorus accompaniment. It does not include the separate vocal or guitar melody. Repeated riffs use consistent chord voicings and bass approaches rather than every small variation or omitted chord tone in the playthrough. The final eight-bar repeat is written/played once; the player can loop it. No claim is made that it reproduces every detail of the original recording.

The initial half note tied to an eighth is held for 2½ quarter-note beats. Bass anticipations tied over bar lines are one continuous note. The generated playback preserves these ties. Score markers move to tied continuation heads without reattacking the sound.

Fingerings are editorial practice suggestions. They are not transcribed fingering instructions from the tutor. The source score is generated by `scripts/build-journey.py`; editing that script and rebuilding keeps notation, playback and the score map together.
