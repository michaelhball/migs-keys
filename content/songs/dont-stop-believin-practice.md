## Practise this excerpt

1. Find B3, just below middle C, and E4 and B4 above it.
2. Play E4 and B4 together with fingers 2 and 5. Then play B3 with your thumb. Allow the hand to move between the two positions.
3. Count **1 & 2 & 3 & 4 &**. The two-note chord falls on each number; the lower B falls on each “&”. Start around 40 quarter notes per minute.
4. Repeat the last pair into the first pair, keeping the same spacing across the bar line. If you hesitate, stop and practise that join separately.
